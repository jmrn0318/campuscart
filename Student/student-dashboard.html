Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Go to Dashboard or Suggestions and click "Add to Cart" on the food you want. Then open Cart and click "Proceed to Checkout". If your cart has items from more than one canteen, each canteen gets its own order.

Why can't I add an item to my cart?

The canteen may be closed right now, or the item is out of stock. Closed canteens are marked on their card. Try again when the canteen reopens, or pick another item.

How do I cancel an order?

In My Orders, select the order and click "Cancel Order". You can cancel while the order is Pending, Confirmed, or Preparing. Once it is Ready for Pickup, please contact the canteen directly.

Can I order the same food again?

Yes. In My Orders, click "Reorder" on an order that is Ready or Completed. Its items go back into your cart, and any that are unavailable are skipped.

How do I suggest a food for a canteen?

Go to Suggestions and click "Share Your Suggestion". Pick a canteen, write what you'd like to see on the menu, and send it. You can follow its status under My Suggestions.

How do I know when my order is ready?

Track it in My Orders: the progress bar moves from Pending to Ready for Pickup. You'll also get an update in Notifications. When it's ready, go to the canteen for pickup.

What does my order code mean?

Every order gets a short code such as CM-1024. Use it to refer to your order when you talk to the canteen staff.

My order is taking too long. What should I do?

Check the estimated time in My Orders first. If it's been longer than expected, ask the canteen staff and give them your order code, or send us a message using the form on this page.

How do I update my profile?

Open Profile and click "Edit Profile" (or any row under Personal Details). You can change your name, student ID, course, and contact number. Your email can't be changed here.

How do I change my profile photo?

In Profile, click the camera button on your picture or "Change Photo". Choose a JPG or PNG under 2 MB. It will be cropped to a square.

How do I change my password?

Click "Change Password" in Profile or Settings. We'll send a reset link to your account email. Open the link and choose a new password.

How do I turn off notifications?

Go to Settings, switch off the alerts you don't want under Notification Preferences, then click "Save Settings".
